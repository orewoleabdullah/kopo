THE PROBLEM

Managing money as a student is already stressful

Most students deal with inconsistent income, peer pressure spending, informal savings systems, poor budgeting habits, and finance apps that feel too serious or complicated.

Kopo is designed to make money feel simpler, social, and less overwhelming.
